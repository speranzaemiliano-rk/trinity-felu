    Vocabulario

    Ordinal numbers · Números ordinales

    Los ordinales sirven para fechas y posiciones: my birthday is on the 5th of May, I'm in the third grade.

    Regla: casi todos terminan en -th. Los que terminan en 1, 2, 3 cambian: -st, -nd, -rd (21st, 22nd, 23rd). Ojo: es twenty-ninth, sin "e".

    Gramática

    Greetings
    • Hello, how are you? — Hola, ¿cómo estás?
    • I'm fine, thank you. How are you? — Bien, gracias. ¿Y vos?
    • Nice to meet you! — ¡Encantado/a!
    • Goodbye, have a nice day! — Chau, ¡que tengas lindo día!
    Al entrar al examen saludá con una sonrisa y al salir decí Goodbye, have a nice day!

    Present simple

    Para rutinas y cosas que pasan siempre. Palabras pista: every day, always, usually, never.

    AfirmativaPreguntaNegativa
    I think.Do I think?I do not (don't) think.
    You think.Do you think?You don't think.
    He thinks.Does he think?He doesn't think.
    She thinks.Does she think?She doesn't think.
    It thinks.Does it think?It doesn't think.
    We think.Do we think?We don't think.
    They think.Do they think?They don't think.
    He, she, it (3ª persona singular): el verbo lleva -s y en preguntas y negativas se usa does / doesn't.
    • I play football every Saturday.
    • She plays the piano. — con he / she / it se agrega -s
    • He watches TV. — después de -ch, -sh, -s, -o se agrega -es
    • I don't like fish. / She doesn't like fish.
    • Do you like pizza? — Yes, I do. / Does he play tennis? — No, he doesn't.
    Present continuous

    Para lo que está pasando ahora. Fórmula: am / is / are + verbo-ing. Pistas: now, at the moment, look!

    AfirmativaNegativaPregunta
    I am going.I am not going.Am I going?
    You are going.You aren't going.Are you going?
    He / She / It is going.He / She / It isn't going.Is he / she / it going?
    We are going.We aren't going.Are we going?
    They are going.They aren't going.Are they going?
    • I am reading a book now.
    • She is running. — run → running (se duplica la consonante)
    • They are playing in the park.
    • What are you doing? — I'm drawing.
    En el examen te pueden mostrar una imagen: What is the boy doing? → He is eating an apple.

    Can (ability)

    Para decir lo que sabés o podés hacer. Va igual para todas las personas, sin -s.

    • I can swim. / She can ride a bike.
    • I can't fly. / He can't cook.
    • Can you dance? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
    Después de can va el verbo solo: she can swim, nunca "she can swims".
    Past tense of the verb to be
    PresentePasadoEjemplo
    I am / he, she, it iswasI was at home yesterday.
    you / we / they arewereThey were at the beach last summer.
    • Negativo: I wasn't tired. / We weren't at school.
    • Pregunta: Were you at the park? — Yes, I was. / Was she happy? — No, she wasn't.
    Pistas de pasado: yesterday, last week, last summer.

    Speaking

    Una charla con el examinador para practicar. Él pregunta en voz alta, el micrófono se activa solo y vos contestás hablando. Si la respuesta queda corta, te da un ejemplo y te deja intentar de nuevo.

    ¿No anda la voz o el micrófono? Probar

    1) Tocá Probar y hablá: la barra tiene que moverse. 2) Después decí "hello, my name is…" y fijate si aparece escrito.

      Examen oral

      Simulacro del Trinity GESE Grade 3: una conversación de unos 5 minutos con el examinador, sin ayudas y con preguntas distintas cada vez. Al final ves la nota: Distinction, Merit o Pass.

      Repaso escrito

      30 preguntas de vocabulario y gramática. Cambian cada vez que empezás.
